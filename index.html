Who we are

Growing minds.
Building character.

Meteitei Boys High School is a public boys’ boarding school in Kamelil, Tinderet Sub-County, Nandi County, Kenya.

Our motto, “Jifunze Uelimike” — learn and be enlightened — expresses the value we place on education as a foundation for personal growth and service to society.

We celebrate the partnership of learners, teachers, parents, alumni and the wider community in creating an environment where every student can pursue excellence.

Our story

A journey of commitment and progress

The school’s story is part of the wider education history of Tinderet and Nandi County.

19 May 1975 — Foundation stone

A historical school profile reports that the foundation stone was laid by Daniel arap Moi, who later became Kenya’s second president. This date refers to the reported foundation-stone ceremony.

1981 — Establishment

Education News reported in March 2025 that the school was established in 1981. The two dates are presented separately because they describe different milestones.

2024 KCSE — A milestone year

Reporting on the 2024 KCSE described the results as the best in the school’s history at that time, with a reported mean score of 8.9.

March 2025 — New facilities and school bus

The school community celebrated the presentation of a Scania-engineered bus and the commissioning of development projects, including a playing field.

Learning at Meteitei

Preparing learners for the future

A moment of pride

Celebrating achievement together

8.9KCSE mean score

Reported overall mean score in the 2024 KCSE examination.

News coverage reported that 431 candidates attained university entry marks. The school community credited the achievement to cooperation among learners, teachers, parents, support staff and the Board of Management.
